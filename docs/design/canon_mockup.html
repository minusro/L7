CANON — GENERATIVE COUNTERPOINT INSTRUMENT

A desktop plugin (JUCE; VST3/AU instrument with MIDI out, plus an AU MIDI-FX build for Logic). A generative arpeggiator/sequencer with a built-in synth: easy to use, complex and evolving output, played live. Polyphony is counterpoint — independent lines, never chords. One screen, no pages. Implementation will live in its own repo; this doc is the UX contract.

THE IDEA: ONE MATERIAL, THREE PLAYHEADS

The engine generates a single melodic line — the material — and three voices are playheads reading that same material, each with its own speed, direction, start offset, octave and scale-degree transpose. A half-speed voice an octave down is augmentation; an offset voice transposed down a fourth is a canon; reverse is retrograde. The classical counterpoint transformations, one knob each. When the generator mutates a note, every voice inherits the change at different times and registers: one mutation ripples through the texture as an evolving motif instead of as noise. Counterpoint comes from shared material, not from voice-leading rules.

THE SCREEN

Header: key/scale, 8 snapshot slots with the A/B morph crossfader, drag-MIDI handle. Material strip: pitch dots + gate bars, padlocked steps, three colored playheads sweeping at their own speeds (the product identity — a screenshot explains the concept). Voice strips: mute, density, speed/direction, octave, phrase, send. Drum strip: mute, density, kick-bass couple, feel, send. Global row: DÉJÀ-VU (the hero knob), drift, mesh, polymetry, FILL, FREEZE.

THE ENGINE

Material → playheads → harmony quantize → per-voice engines; drums run their own rhythm material on the same mutation clock and skip the harmony stage.

  • Déjà-vu — the hero knob. A seeded stream feeds all lanes; the knob morphs pure random → looping with occasional mutations → fully locked loop. One knob is the whole learning curve, and it governs the melodic and drum material on one clock.
  • Lanes — pitch, gate, velocity, octave are separate lanes over the material's steps. A single polymetry macro progressively de-synchronizes the lane lengths along a curated sequence (16/16/16/16 → 15/16/13/16 → …), so even a fully locked loop keeps recombining — deterministic evolution, free.
  • Harmony lane — an invisible root that drifts on a very long loop (4–16 bars). Pitch quantization weights chord tones on strong beats, allows passing tones on weak ones; the drift knob sets how adventurous the root movement is. Nothing out-of-key can ever come out.
  • Register bands — each voice folds into its own octave band; lines never cross, the texture stays legible.
  • Mesh — each voice's fire probability dips just after another voice fires. Low = voices stack; high = call-and-response, hocket.

THE PHRASE KNOB (per voice)

The material stores a gate value per step (0…1; 0 is a rest), written and mutated by the generator. The knob morphs between ignoring and honoring it:

effective gatelerp(arp gate, stored gate, PHRASE)
tiethe note extends into the next step when effective gate ≥ 1.0
at 0uniform retriggered arp notes
turning upthe longest stored gates cross the tie threshold one at a time — notes merge into phrases progressively, never all at once
at 1full stored phrasing: a ½× playhead sings the material as long vocal lines while the 2× playhead runs it as filigree

Deterministic and click-free. Per voice (bass arpy, lead legato is exactly the differentiation counterpoint wants); a global macro may offset all three.

DRUMS

A fourth voice group: four rhythm/velocity lanes (kick, snare, hat, perc), no pitch, its own material — mutating on the same déjà-vu clock. The drum material stays invisible: drums are knob-sculpted (density, couple, feel), not step-edited. Glue: one shared groove (swing + accent template) across drums and melody; a kick↔bass couple knob (at full, the bass voice fires only with/against the kick); a FILL button — momentary density + ratchet surge, bar-quantized, snaps back on release.

PERFORMANCE

  • Freeze captures the current loop into one of 8 snapshot slots — happy accidents are never lost. Snapshots store knob state and material.
  • A/B crossfader: pick two snapshots, ride the fader between them — knobs and material interpolate. A performance arc in one gesture, and it can be ridden back and forth.
  • Material strip is touchable: tap a step to lock it against mutation (random until good, then protected), drag to re-pitch. Per-step mutation locks is something no Turing-machine descendant has.
  • All voice/global moves are bar-quantized so tweaks land musically; every control is MIDI-mappable and host-automatable.
  • Playhead speeds are musical ratios (½, ¾, 1, 3/2, 2…); a LOOSE toggle (off by default) frees the ratios for Reich-style phasing.

SOUND & MIDI

  • Per-voice synth engine, Plaits family (MIT): three melodic instances + percussion models for the drums — one synth family, sounds good immediately, macro-friendly (model / harmonics / timbre / morph). Not a destination page.
  • One shared FX bus: tempo-synced delay + reverb, per-voice sends, two knobs. The delay is secretly structural — a dotted-eighth send is another pseudo-playhead.
  • MIDI out: V1/V2/V3 on channels 1–3, drums on 10 — one instance drives three hardware synths and a drum machine.
  • Drag-out MIDI: drag the current frozen loop from the plugin window into the DAW as a multitrack MIDI file, one track per voice. Sidesteps all host routing pain.

DECIDED

  • Counterpoint from shared material: one line, three playheads + drums. Never chords.
  • Déjà-vu locked randomness as the core; freeze + 8 snapshots; A/B crossfader morph.
  • Polymetry as one macro, not per-lane length boxes.
  • Invisible drifting harmony lane with chord-tone weighting; register bands per voice.
  • Material strip visible and touchable (tap-lock, drag re-pitch); drum material invisible.
  • Phrase knob per voice: gate lerp with tie threshold.
  • Speeds locked to musical ratios; LOOSE toggle off by default.
  • Per-voice Plaits-family engines; shared delay+reverb bus with sends.
  • MIDI: voices on ch 1–3, drums ch 10; drag-out multitrack MIDI.
  • Presets store the material itself, not seed + replay — freezes are bit-exact forever.
  • Base rate: material step = 16th at 1×, global ÷2/×2.
  • Name: Canon.
