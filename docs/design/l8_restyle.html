L8 Screen Restyle

The looper's 320×240 screen redrawn in the style of Elektron's monochrome displays, with the same layout. Each image is rendered from the firmware's own drawing code on a PC (tools/ui_preview), shown here at 2×.

Before and after

What changed

  • Palette: black, 25% grey (background detail: outside the loop range, fills, unlit arcs, the row's other frames), 50% grey (inactive: muted clips, idle icons and text), white (active, selected), and an amber accent for recording and a scene change on its way. The accent is one constant in colors.h.
  • Grid: everything on 2×2-pixel blocks; cells moved by a pixel or two so they sit on the grid, gaps one block wide, the button now square.
  • Selection: a white frame on the current cell, dim frames on the rest of the row, the row's button inverted.
  • Waveforms: a centre line with peaks, white inside the loop range and dim outside; the range bracket, start line and bar ticks on the selected track.
  • Text: a 3×5 pixel font (the splash screen's), and boxed tags for SCN and the tempo being edited.

Every state

Preview data is made up: synthetic waveforms, meter levels and a scene list that's empty.
